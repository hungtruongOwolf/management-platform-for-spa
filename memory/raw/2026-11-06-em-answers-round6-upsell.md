## Lead's reading
- **Q-U1:** case by case.
  - If the new package covers the service done today, the desk may choose to count today as session 1 and reverse the one-off charge.
  - Otherwise (unrelated package) it is just a new package sale and today's one-off stays.
  - Never automatic; the default is "keep today's one-off".
- **Q-U2:** one "người giới thiệu / chốt" per sale. Choices: chủ spa, quản lý, a KTV (any staff), or "khách tự chọn" (nobody is credited).
- **Q-U3 + Q-U4 note:** the upsell commission (= compensation-rules §3.2 "chốt", same %) applies to packages AND one-off services sold by referral. The "làm" commission (§3.1) goes to whoever performs the service. These are two separate credits, possibly to two different people.
- **Q-U4:** accepted as proposed.
- **Q-U5:** accepted.
  - Buying for someone else: the sale records the payer, the package belongs to the user.
  - Plus: a companion who is not served, or a family member, can be added as a note only ("chồng của …", "gia đình của …") without creating a customer record.
  - In group booking/check-in, a person may be "ghi chú" instead of a customer.
- **Q-U7:** deferred.
  - Customers normally pay in full.
  - When added later: a debtor cannot use sessions, and there is a reminder list of unpaid sales.
- **Q-U6, Q-U8, Q-U9, Q-E1, Q-E2:** pasted back with no objection. Read as accepted as proposed.
