# EM decision — 2026-11-08: technicians get their own evaluation history

After PR #90 the lead asked whether technicians, who browse their past months with the month picker in "Công và lương của tôi", should also get a 12-month history screen of their own. Verbatim:

> cũng ổn làm nốt feature đó đi

## Lead's reading
The current behaviour is acceptable, but the EM wants the feature finished.
- "Công và lương của tôi" gains the signed-in staff member's own evaluation history: a 12-month trend plus one row per month (result, praise/complaints, leave, late, "Xem phiếu"). It is the same layout as the owner and manager history in #90, limited to the caller's own linked staff profile.
- Former staff see only the months they were employed (ADR-0014).
