## Lead's reading
1. **Management / review views are missing.** The app is entry-oriented. EM wants to *see*:
   - today's totals (how many customers today, how much money);
   - a customer's full usage history;
   - generally, follow-up and oversight views.

   Paper books to mirror: `docs for reference/sheet cho quản lý khách hàng/` (sổ theo dõi doanh thu hằng ngày, sổ theo dõi liệu trình khách hàng, thu chi).
2. **No "sổ cũ vs sổ mới" distinction in the product.** Paper-book data is a one-time data migration the team fills in later. So:
   - remove the user-facing concept: the /nhap-so-cu nav item and screen for staff, the "Nhập từ sổ cũ" badges and labels, and the home tile;
   - migrated packages look like any other package.
3. **Use the real logo:** `docs for reference/logo Viên phúc y đường transparent.png`. It is a gold circle with a lotus on top, the script wordmark "Viên Phúc Y Đường", a yin-yang with leaves, and the curved tagline "Hồi sinh sức khoẻ và sắc đẹp". The file only exists as a glowing raster.
4. **UI still "rất tệ".** EM wants it beautiful, easy to manage, with effects like a professional website. The brand-colour pass (PR #27) is not enough.
